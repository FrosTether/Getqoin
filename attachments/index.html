How it works

  1. Install Graysons WalletAndroid 8.0 or newer. It adds three apps: Graysons Wallet, Frostoise and MyFrost. Your phone will ask you to allow the install, since it isn't on the Play Store.
    Download the APK
  2. Create your walletTap Create wallet and write down your 25 words. They are the only way to get your coins back. Never share them.
  3. Connect to the networkPhones on the same Wi-Fi find each other. Otherwise, open Node → Add and enter a public node from the project discussions.
  4. Claim your name and payTap Claim name, then pay with Chime or Dogecoin and send me your .frostchain name or Receive address.
  5. Get your QOINI send 10 QOIN to your name. It shows up once the next block is mined.

Want to mine instead?

Frostoise mines while your phone hears a pulsed 7.83 Hz tone from any speaker. See the tone mining guide.
