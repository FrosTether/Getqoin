How it works

  1. Install Graysons WalletAndroid 8.0 or newer. One install adds three apps: Graysons Wallet, Frostoise, and MyFrost. The phone will ask you to allow the install, because it isn’t on the Play Store.
    Download the APK
  2. Create your walletTap Create wallet and write down your 25 words. They are the only way to get the coins back. Never share them.
  3. Connect to the networkPhones on the same Wi-Fi find each other. Otherwise open Node → Add and enter a public node from the project discussions.
  4. Claim your name and payTap Claim name, pay with Chime or Dogecoin, and send me your .frostchain name or Receive address.
  5. Get your QOINI send 10 QOIN to that name. It shows up once the next block is mined.

Want to mine instead?

Frostoise mines while the phone hears a pulsed 7.83 Hz tone from any speaker. See the tone mining guide.
